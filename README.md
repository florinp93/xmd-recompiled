📖 The Project
This project is a dedicated, community-driven effort to bring the 2011 action RPG X-Men Destiny to modern Windows PCs. Instead of relying on traditional console emulators, which can be resource-heavy and prone to graphical glitches, this project translates the original game code so it runs completely natively on your computer. The goal is to create the definitive, playable PC version of a game that was entirely left behind by the industry.

🎮 About the Game
Released in 2011 and developed by Silicon Knights, X-Men Destiny puts you in the shoes of a brand-new, customizable mutant in a world where anti-mutant tensions are boiling over. Following the death of Professor X, you must navigate a fractured San Francisco and decide the fate of mutant-kind.

Instead of playing as an established comic character, you forge your own path:

Choose Your Origin: Select from three unique protagonists, each with their own backstory.

Manifest Your Powers: Pick from distinct mutant ability trees (Density Control, Energy Projection, or Shadow Matter).

Pick a Side: Align yourself with the ideological beliefs of either the X-Men or the Brotherhood of Mutants.

Equip X-Genes: Collect and equip genetic enhancements from famous Marvel mutants like Wolverine, Magneto, and Gambit to customize your combat style.

🛑 Why Does This Project Exist?
X-Men Destiny is essentially lost media. Shortly after its release, a massive legal dispute between developer Silicon Knights and Epic Games resulted in a court order mandating the recall and physical destruction of all unsold copies of the game. It was permanently delisted from digital storefronts and never saw a release beyond the PS3, Xbox 360, and Wii.

Because it was erased from the market, it never received a PC port or any post-launch preservation. This project exists to rescue X-Men Destiny from the void of video game history. By porting it natively to PC, we are ensuring that Marvel fans, video game historians, and action RPG players can actually access and experience this unique chapter of X-Men gaming without needing to hunt down a rare, surviving disc and a 15-year-old console.
